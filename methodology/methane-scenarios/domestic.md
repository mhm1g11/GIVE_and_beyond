# Domestic methane emissions

## Oil and gas

Document the asset boundary, annual operating activity, fugitive emissions, venting, flaring and corresponding mitigation technologies. Identify the applicable leak detection and repair, monitoring and equipment-replacement assumptions, together with their implementation timing.

## Coal

Treat active coal mines and closed, inactive or abandoned mines separately. For each class, specify the relevant production or asset inventory, methane factors, capture or destruction potential, and the assumptions governing decline after mine closure. Do not transfer active-mine intensity rules to abandoned assets without justification.

## Aggregation and overlap

Domestic reductions may be added to import-related reductions only when emissions and asset boundaries are mutually exclusive. Track geography, accounting year, sector, units, source version and abatement ownership.

## Evidence required before numerical publication

For each fuel/technology combination, add an auditable table of baseline emissions, intervention, emissions after intervention, absolute reduction, source, uncertainty and a reproducible code link.

The published GIVE fork currently provides the **valuation interface** for pathways. A domestic asset-to-pathway pipeline requires separate documentation and validation.
